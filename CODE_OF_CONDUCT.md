# Code of conduct

## The standard

Be respectful and assume good faith. Disagree about ideas, not about people.

This project handles situations where people are often stressed, out of money, or
being treated unfairly. That context should show in how we talk about users: no
condescension about what someone should have read before signing, and no jokes at
the expense of people in difficulty.

## Not acceptable

Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language, publishing others' private
information, and sustained disruption.

## Legal content

A wrong citation in this codebase can send someone to the wrong forum or past a
deadline. Correcting legal content is always welcome and should never be taken
personally — including corrections to work that has already been merged.

Do not use issues or pull requests to seek legal advice for your own situation.

## Enforcement

Report concerns by opening an issue or contacting a maintainer privately.
Maintainers may edit, remove or reject contributions that do not meet this
standard, and may block repeat offenders.
